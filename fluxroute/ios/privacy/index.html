FluxRoute · iOS

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 July 2026 · Applies to: FluxRoute for iOS

In short: FluxRoute needs no account. Your game progress stays on your device. The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, which may use a device advertising identifier — personalized only if you allow tracking. We also use Google Firebase for anonymous analytics and crash reports. We don’t sell your personal data.

1. Who we are

FluxRoute (“the app”) is developed and operated by Erge Akova (“we”, “us”, “our”), the data controller for the limited data described below. Contact: dev.ergeakova@gmail.com. Jurisdiction: Türkiye.

2. Information we collect and process

a) Data stored on your device (not transmitted to us)

Gameplay and preferences are saved locally on your device and are not sent to us: level progress and stars, coins, hints, streaks and freezes, missions, season/league points, unlocked themes and arrow skins, daily reward state, selected language, and settings (sound, music, haptics, appearance, color-blind mode, reminder time, notification preference). We have no server and cannot see this data.

b) Advertising data (Google AdMob)

The app is free and ad-supported through Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC). To serve, cap, and measure ads, AdMob and its partners may collect and process:

  • A device advertising identifier (Apple IDFA) and/or Google advertising ID;
  • IP address and derived approximate (coarse) location (e.g., country/region);
  • Device information — device model, operating system version, language, and similar technical data;
  • Ad interaction data — ads shown, clicks, and diagnostics needed to prevent fraud and abuse.

Personalized ads are shown only if you grant permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt. If you decline, ads are non-personalized (contextual). Learn more: How Google uses information · Google Privacy Policy.

c) Game Center (Apple)

If you use leaderboards or achievements, Apple’s Game Center processes your Game Center player identifier, display name, scores, and achievement progress. This is handled by Apple under its Privacy Policy. Game Center is optional.

d) Analytics, crash reporting & configuration (Google Firebase)

The app uses Google Firebase (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC). These services process a random, app-generated app-instance identifier — not your name, email or Apple ID:

  • Firebase Analytics — anonymous usage events such as which levels are started, completed or failed, scores and combos, daily-streak milestones, mission and reward interactions, and which ad formats were shown. Used to understand how the game is played and to improve it.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash and error reports: stack traces, device model, operating system version and app version, so we can find and fix bugs.
  • Firebase Remote Config — fetches configuration values (for example ad frequency and feature flags). It does not collect personal data beyond the app-instance identifier.

Apple may additionally provide us aggregate, anonymized diagnostics if you opted in to share analytics with developers in your iOS settings. Learn more: Firebase privacy & security · Google Privacy Policy.

What we do NOT collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, precise GPS location, or health data. The app has no login or user account.

4. Legal bases (EEA / UK)

Where the GDPR/UK GDPR applies, we rely on: legitimate interests to operate the game and show non-personalized ads and to keep the service secure; and your consent (via the ATT prompt and any consent form) for personalized advertising and the use of the advertising identifier for tracking. You can withdraw consent at any time (see section 6).

5. Sharing & third parties

We do not sell your personal data. Data described above is processed by:

  • Google (AdMob advertising; Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and Remote Config) — policy.
  • Apple (Game Center, App Store, iOS diagnostics) — policy.

These providers may process data on servers located in other countries; they implement their own safeguards for international transfers (e.g., Standard Contractual Clauses).

6. Your choices & rights

Everyone

  • Turn personalized ads off/on: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Delete all on-device data: delete the app.

EEA / UK (GDPR)

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, or object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Because we hold almost no data tied to your identity, most requests are handled via the device controls above; for anything else contact dev.ergeakova@gmail.com.

California (CCPA/CPRA)

You have the right to know, delete, and opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information. We do not sell data; showing personalized ads may be considered “sharing”, which you can opt out of by declining the tracking prompt. You will not be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

7. Data retention

On-device data is kept until you delete the app. Advertising identifiers are controlled by iOS and can be reset or limited in Settings. Data processed by Google and Apple is retained per their policies.

8. Security

Gameplay data is stored locally on your device. We take reasonable measures to protect the limited data involved, but no method of storage or transmission is 100% secure.

9. Children’s privacy

FluxRoute is rated 4+ and contains no objectionable content. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will address it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected here with a new “Last updated” date. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
