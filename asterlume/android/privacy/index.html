Asterlume · Android

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 July 2026 · Applies to the Asterlume app for Android

In short
  • Asterlume has no account. We never ask for your name, email, or contacts.
  • Your game progress is stored only on your device — we run no servers of our own.
  • The app shows ads (Google AdMob) and uses analytics and crash reporting (Google Firebase). These collect limited device data, described below.
  • Where the law requires it (EEA, UK, Switzerland), a Google consent form (User Messaging Platform) appears before any ad loads — and no ad service starts until you answer. You can decline and still play everything.
  • We never sell your personal data.

1. Who we are

Asterlume (“we”, “us”) is a calm constellation puzzle game for Android, developed by Erge Akova. This policy explains what data is processed when you use the app and what choices you have. Questions: dev.ergeakova@gmail.com.

2. Data we do not collect

  • No account or registration — we do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or passwords.
  • No precise location. The app never requests GPS access.
  • No access to your contacts, microphone, camera, or existing photos.
  • Your puzzle progress, streaks, and settings are stored locally on your device (app storage) and are never uploaded to any server we operate. We do not operate any backend of our own.

3. Data collected by third-party services

Asterlume uses a small number of Google services. They automatically collect certain device data when you use the app.

Google AdMob (advertising)

Asterlume is free and supported by ads. To serve and measure ads, Google AdMob may collect:

  • Device identifiers, including the Android advertising ID (AAID) — used for personalized ads only with your consent (see Section 4);
  • IP address and coarse, city-level location derived from it;
  • Device information (model, operating system, language);
  • Ad interaction data (which ads were shown, viewed, or tapped).

Details: Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

Google Firebase Analytics (usage analytics)

We use anonymous usage analytics to understand how the game is played and to improve it — for example which levels are started and completed, how long puzzles take, and which settings are used. Firebase Analytics collects a random app-instance identifier and general device information. These events do not include your name or any content that identifies you personally.

Google Firebase Crashlytics (crash reporting)

If the app crashes, a crash report is sent so we can find and fix the bug. Reports include the crash stack trace, device model, operating-system version, and the device state at the time of the crash. They do not include personal information.

Google Firebase Remote Config (app configuration)

Used to adjust game settings (such as feature availability and ad pacing) without an app update. It uses the same app-instance identifier as Firebase Analytics and does not collect additional personal data.

4. Advertising consent and your choices

Consent form (EEA, UK, and Switzerland). If you are in a region covered by the GDPR, Asterlume presents Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form the first time you open the app, before any ad is requested. The advertising SDK is not initialized and makes no ad-related network request until your consent choice is recorded — if consent is unknown or declined, no ad service starts. You can choose personalized ads, non-personalized ads, or decline, and in every case you can still play the entire game.

Changing your choice. Where a consent form is required, you can reopen it any time from Settings → Privacy inside the app (the “Ad privacy options” / “Reklam tercihleri” entry, shown only in regions where it applies) to update or withdraw your consent.

Personalized vs. non-personalized ads. Personalized ads use your device’s Android advertising ID (AAID) to tailor ads to your interests; non-personalized ads do not. To reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization at the device level, go to Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) on your device.

5. Permissions

  • Notifications (Android 13+): if you enable them, the app schedules local reminders on your device (for example, a daily puzzle reminder). We do not operate a push server, and no data is sent anywhere to deliver these reminders. You can turn them off in Android Settings at any time.
  • Vibration: used only for in-game haptic feedback.
  • Saving wallpapers: Asterlume can save constellation wallpapers you create to your device gallery. This is add-only — the app can save new images but cannot read, browse, or upload your existing photos. On older Android versions this uses a storage permission scoped to writing images; on newer versions it uses the system media store. Wallpapers are generated on your device and never leave it.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with anyone except the Google services listed in Section 3, which process it to provide advertising, analytics, and crash reporting. Google’s processing is governed by its own privacy policy.

7. Data retention

We do not store personal data on our own servers. Data collected by Google services is retained according to Google’s retention policies — for example, Firebase Analytics data is retained for a limited period before being deleted or aggregated. Game progress stored on your device remains until you delete the app or reset progress in the app’s settings.

8. Data deletion

  • No account means there is no server-side profile to delete.
  • On-device data: uninstalling the app, or using Settings → reset progress inside the app, removes all locally stored game data.
  • Ad and analytics data: you can reset your advertising ID and opt out of personalization in your device settings (Section 4). For any additional request, email dev.ergeakova@gmail.com and we will help, including forwarding requests to Google where applicable.

9. Children’s privacy

Asterlume is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). It is not enrolled in Google Play’s “Designed for Families” program. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the app, contact us at dev.ergeakova@gmail.com and we will take appropriate steps.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your personal data. Because we do not hold personal data ourselves, most requests are best handled through your device controls:

  • Change your ad consent in the app (Settings → Privacy) where applicable;
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings;
  • Delete the app to remove all locally stored game data.

For anything else, email dev.ergeakova@gmail.com and we will help, including forwarding requests to our service providers where applicable. We do not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.

11. International data transfers

The Google services we use may process data on servers located in countries other than yours, including the United States. Those transfers are handled by Google under its own safeguards and privacy commitments described in the Google Privacy Policy linked above.

12. Security

Data transmitted by the third-party SDKs is sent over encrypted connections (TLS). Game data on your device is protected by Android’s standard app-sandbox isolation. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we rely on established Google SDKs and platform protections.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy — for example when adding new features — we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will be noted in the app where appropriate.

14. Contact

Questions or requests: dev.ergeakova@gmail.com
